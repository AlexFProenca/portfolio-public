Privacy Policy

MindHexOps · Last updated 23 October 2026

In one sentence

MindHexOps collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and stores your data only on your own computer.

What the app stores, and where

MindHexOps keeps everything it needs in a single folder on your own device, at %APPDATA%\MindHexOps\. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

  • messages.txt — the messages you type into the app
  • Images\ — the image files you choose to add
  • autoStart.config — whether you asked the app to start with Windows

These files never leave your computer. You can open, edit, copy or delete them at any time using Windows Explorer.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind.
  • We do not use telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.
  • We do not require an account, a sign-in, an email address or any registration.
  • We do not transmit your messages, your images or any other data over the internet.
  • We do not share or sell anything to third parties, because we hold nothing to share.
  • We do not use cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files outside the app's own folder.

MindHexOps makes no network connections at all. It works identically with the computer offline.

Data deletion

To remove every trace of the app's data, delete the folder %APPDATA%\MindHexOps\. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. There is no server-side copy to request, because no server ever received anything.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, of any age. There is nothing to consent to, because nothing is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new date. Because the app is designed to work without any data collection, we do not expect it to change in any way that affects your privacy.
