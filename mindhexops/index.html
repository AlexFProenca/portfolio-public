Program your own mind.

MindHexOps is a minimalist Windows app that puts your own messages and images on screen, on a schedule, over any window you're working in.

What it does

Your words, on screen

Type your messages, one per line. They appear as topmost overlays with a smooth fade in and out, then repeat.

Your images too

Drop in any JPG or PNG. MindHexOps shows them the same way — a goal, a number, a face, whatever you need in front of you.

Out of the way

Runs from the system tray. Optional start with Windows, launching minimised. Never interrupts you with popups.

Entirely yours

No account, no sign-in, no telemetry, no cloud. Your messages are a plain text file on your own disk.

Honest about what it is

It is

  • A reminder and reinforcement tool you configure yourself
  • A way to keep a goal, a number or an image in your field of view
  • A small, quiet Windows utility that stays out of the way

It is not

  • A magic switch — you still do the work
  • A cloud service, a subscription, or an account
  • Anything that sends your data anywhere

Technical

Self-contained .NET 9 build. Extract the download and run MindHexOps.exe — there is no installer, no runtime to install and no setup wizard. Your messages and images are stored in %APPDATA%\MindHexOps\, so they survive updates.

Requires Windows 10 (build 19041 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. The app is currently unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may show a one-time warning on first run: choose More info → Run anyway.
