# Roteiro — Dealing with Difficult Americans

## Overview
Rafael just arrived in New York. He will face 7 situations with classic American personalities.
Every choice has 3 options: correct, reasonable, wrong. Results change tone and sometimes final ending.

## Act 1 — The Bodega
- Location: Queens corner store
- Character: Cashier Karen
- Theme: Politely declining / standing your ground

## Act 2 — The Gas Station
- Location: Jersey highway pump
- Character: American Idiot
- Theme: Asking for help / resisting pressure

## Act 3 — The Street
- Location: Manhattan sidewalk
- Character: Cop
- Theme: Handling criticism / controlling the moment

## Act 4 — The Café
- Location: Brooklyn coffee shop
- Character: Woogirl
- Theme: Asking for help / ending an awkward conversation

## Act 5 — The Pharmacy
- Location: Duane Reade
- Character: Cashier Karen
- Theme: Explaining a mistake

## Act 6 — The Gas Station Again
- Location: Same pump
- Character: American Idiot
- Theme: Resisting pressure politely after repeat contact

## Act 7 — The Final Stop
- Location: Street corner
- Character: Cop
- Theme: Ending an awkward interaction cleanly
